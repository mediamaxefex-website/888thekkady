The meaning behind 888

Inspired by the highlands. Chosen for good energy.

Kumily and Thekkady sit in Kerala’s beautiful highland country, often described around 880–890 metres above sea level. We chose 888 as a memorable symbol of balance, abundance and positive beginnings—the spirit we hope you feel throughout your stay.
